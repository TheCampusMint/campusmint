# iPhone functional checklist — October 31, 2026

Use the new iPhone build and https://www.thecampusmint.com. Sign in as the same account on both; use a second test account for messages and privacy checks. These boxes are unverified manual checks, not completed results. Visual comparison is yours to judge.

## New cross-device sync

- [ ] **Messages:** leave a conversation open on the iPhone. Send from the other account on the website. The message arrives without reopening or pulling down. Reply from the iPhone; it appears once on the website.
- [ ] **Seen/unread:** reading a thread updates its unread count and the sender's seen state. Open/close it several times; no repeated messages or endless loading.
- [ ] **Comments:** add/reply/like a comment on the website while the same post's comments are open on the iPhone. The change arrives automatically. Delete your comment and confirm it disappears on both.
- [ ] **History:** load older messages/comments, then make a new change on the website. Older pages remain available, with no duplicate rows. Deleted comments do not remain in older history.
- [ ] **Notifications/pins:** mark a notification read or pin/unpin a conversation on one device. Confirm the same state on the other.
- [ ] **Reconnect:** briefly disable the iPhone's connection, make a change on the website, then reconnect. The open screen catches up. Normally changes arrive promptly; allow about 30 seconds for the fallback when realtime is unavailable.
- [ ] **Foreground:** switch away from the app, make a change on the website, then return. It refreshes without requiring a restart. Background push alerts are not part of this checkpoint.
- [ ] **Account isolation:** log out, then sign into another test account. None of the old account's messages, notifications or drafts appear, including after a delayed request completes.

## Existing app behavior to compare

- [ ] **Sign-in/profile:** restarting preserves the same account, username and profile. Logout removes private screens. Native profile editing currently opens the website.
- [ ] **Feed:** posts and media load, preserve shape, and open comments. A shared public post link opens outside the app without sign-in; private posts remain protected.
- [ ] **Composer:** plain text, photo/video and poll posts publish successfully and appear on the website. Blank text cannot post. Default visibility is Public and duration is Permanent; changed privacy/duration is honored.
- [ ] **Drafts/retry:** save, restart and reopen a draft with its attachments. Retry after an interrupted upload; exactly one post appears. Drafts are currently local to the iPhone, not cloud-synced.
- [ ] **Media/quality:** portrait/landscape images and video keep their shape and orientation. HD/4K starts off, remembers your choice, and affects newly selected media. Published clips play on both clients.
- [ ] **Polls:** vote, reopen and compare totals with the website. Invalid/duplicate options cannot publish.
- [ ] **Sports:** compare the same university's sports, season records, upcoming dates and favorites with the website. Future-year games remain upcoming.
- [ ] **Clubs:** Discover/My clubs show the correct campus and memberships; private details remain restricted. Club creation/management currently hands off to the website.
- [ ] **Search:** Food/Events/Sell load, search and refresh. Restaurant information is limited until the Places key is configured. Native discovery currently uses campus coordinates, not live device location.
- [ ] **Marketplace:** listing details match the website. Posting/contact currently hands off to the website; a complete native listing/contact flow is still pending.
- [ ] **Appearance:** Light/Dark/Colorful and the selected accent persist after restart. Check composer, settings and navigation as part of your own visual review.
- [ ] **Usability:** keyboard dismissal, scrolling, outside-tap composer dismissal, text size, VoiceOver labels and Reduced Motion behave acceptably.

## Do not count these as finished native parity

Native club management, marketplace posting/contact, profile editing, resolved mentions, event/club composer attachments, camera capture, live-location discovery, all likes/saves/follows/block/attendance controls, DM media attachments, licensed GIF/sticker search and background push delivery still require work. Music stays absent. Apple linking, production App Attest, subscriptions/8K and SMS stay disabled as agreed.

For an issue, report the checklist item, app or website, account/campus, steps, expected result and actual result. Avoid sending tokens or private message content.
