# iPhone feed/history checklist — November 2, 2026

Use the new app build and the same account on the website. These are pending user checks, not claimed device passes. The previous full checklist remains at `2026-11-01-iphone-functional-checklist.md`.

- [ ] Open your profile and another person's profile. Confirm their posts appear even when those posts aren't in the home feed.
- [ ] On an account with enough posts, tap More posts repeatedly. Older posts should load without duplicate cards; refresh should recover from an expired page or temporary network failure.
- [ ] From a second account, change access or block the viewer. Confirm affected posts disappear from the iPhone, including older loaded history. Accepted connections should see eligible connections-only posts; unilateral followers should not.
- [ ] Read another person's post for at least 8 seconds, keep the app foregrounded for a further 30 seconds, then refresh the website later. Recommendations may gradually reflect the topic; this does not promise a fixed next post or an instant visible reorder.
- [ ] Scroll quickly, open the composer, switch tabs, or background the app. These should not keep counting as reading the covered post.
- [ ] Like/endorse and use Not interested. Confirm dismissed posts stay out of the home feed after loading more and refreshing. Direct profile/history browsing intentionally remains available where permissions allow it.
- [ ] Go offline briefly, return online, and refresh. Failed reads should offer recovery; no other account's posts or reading state should survive logout/sign-in.

If iOS still refuses to open the Personal Team build, check the development profile's trust first. A successful signed build is not proof of a successful physical-device launch.
